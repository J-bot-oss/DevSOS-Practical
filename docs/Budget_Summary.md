# DevSOS Project Budget

The editable workbook is stored locally as `docs/local/DevSOS_Project_Budget.xlsx`. It contains Project Budget, Actual Expenditure, Budget Monitoring and Budget Assumptions sheets.

These are provisional estimates from the Project Charter, not approved funding or actual expenditure. The budget retains the project period of **18 November to 1 December 2026**, three website pages and four troubleshooting guides.

## Itemization and assumptions

Development data is split into a UGX 15,000 website allowance and a UGX 10,000 guide research and verification allowance. Communication/testing is split into UGX 4,000 for coordination and UGX 6,000 for learner trials and testing connectivity. Each uses a quantity of one allowance. These splits are unverified planning assumptions, not supplier quotes or confirmed data bundles.

Existing equipment and free tools are assumed. No new hardware, paid hosting, paid domain or wage is included. Proposed allowance dates follow schedule activities and support a simple planned-cost comparison. They are not actual transaction dates. Category subtotal formulas reconcile the itemized estimates with the charter.

## Actual costs and monitoring

The expenditure register is blank. It provides fields for date, budget item ID, description, amount, evidence reference, payment status and notes. Record the full cost once, including unpaid charges; payment status is tracked separately. Keep supporting records and payment details local.

Actual totals, variances and remaining allowance stay blank until a complete reconciliation has an evidence reference and the input checks are clear. An empty register does not establish zero spending. An evidenced, complete review can explicitly confirm zero costs. Remaining allowance is a planning-budget balance, not cash available or proof of funding.

The contingency is included **once** in the UGX 50,000 total. Supported reserve allocations are recorded separately, with the cost item, reason, decision and evidence. Costs funded from the reserve are entered against a normal cost item with the allocation ID. Reserve spending is a subset of actual cost and is never added again as a second expense.

## Progress reports

The workbook retains planned report dates of **24 November and 30 November 2026** from the Project Schedule. The proposed cost phasing gives UGX 29,000 planned by the first report and UGX 35,000 by the second, excluding the unallocated reserve. Actual report dates, actual costs, review evidence, issues and corrective actions remain blank.

Variance is planned cost less recorded actual cost. A negative amount indicates costs above the comparison allowance. Comparisons use each report's cutoff date and require a supported reconciliation.

Sources: Project Charter draft version 0.1, approved WBS and current Project Schedule baseline. No confidential evidence, personal identifiers, payment details or stakeholder statements are included in this summary.
